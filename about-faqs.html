The 4-stage operating system

We operate through a sequential protocol inspired by cleanroom standards, preventing cross-contamination while ensuring repeatable high-grade perfection.

Protocol phase 1

Zone assessment & priority plan

Upon entry, team leads inspect flooring substrates, fragile stone, pet zones, and high-frequency touch surfaces to sequence the cleaning path and isolate priority tasks.

Protocol phase 2

HEPA filtration & color microfiber

4-stage 99.97% HEPA filtration sequesters airborne particulates. Color-coded cloths (Red: sanitary fixtures, Blue: glass/metals, Green: prep areas) prevent bacterial transfer.

palette Strict 3-color isolation system
03 vertical_align_bottom
Protocol phase 3

Top-to-bottom multi-surface sanitizing

Cleaners work downward from crown molding, fans, and light fixtures down to counters and baseboards. Dust is captured damp, followed by microfiber mop finishing.

layers Gravity-sequential dusting

A designated lead audits light switches, cabinet trim, baseboard creases, and air grills against our 35-point checklist before signing off and releasing the premises.

Frequently asked questions

Transparent policies regarding products, access security, quality guarantees, and recurring care tiers.

We exclusively utilize eco-friendly, non-toxic formulations certified by Green Seal and EPA Safer Choice. Our plant-derived enzyme detergents and citric-based mineral removers are thoroughly safe for households with young infants, sensitive elderly residents, and indoor pets. For delicate materials such as unsealed quartzite, honed limestone, and aged bronze, we apply custom neutral-pH formulas that clean without etching or degrading natural sealers.
No presence is required. Over 75% of our residential and commercial clients prefer our vetted secure access protocol.
